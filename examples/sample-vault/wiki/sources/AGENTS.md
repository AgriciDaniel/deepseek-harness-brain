## Compiled Truth

- New behavior belongs on documented plugin extension points.
- Registrations are reversible effects and must prove disposal.
- Model-visible data must be reconstructable from the session log.
- A capability requires definition, provider, and consumer roles.
- Verification must match the affected surface and must not inflate skipped or static evidence.
