# Capability seams and core services

## What It Says

- The generated capability graph distinguishes service declarations, providers, and direct consumers.
- Provider replacement changes all consumers behind the same service interface.
- Filesystem, subprocess, sandbox, approval, LLM, persistence, and subagents are separate services.
