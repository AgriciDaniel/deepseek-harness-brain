# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a respectful place for careful technical disagreement.
Contributors are expected to:

- Critique claims and evidence, not people.
- Separate verified facts, inference, practitioner advice, and open questions.
- Welcome corrections and disclose uncertainty.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and publication
  of another person's private information.
- Respect upstream maintainers, contributors, licenses, and trademarks.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a private report, contact the maintainer through a private method listed on
the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel). Do not post
personal details or sensitive evidence in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, limit participation,
or permanently ban repeated or severe violations. Reports will be reviewed as
privately and promptly as practical, but this volunteer project offers no formal
response-time guarantee.

## Attribution

This concise policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted to
the evidence-review nature of this repository.
